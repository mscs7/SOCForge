SOCForge is a functional local SOC portfolio lab. It provides deterministic risk scoring and MITRE ATT&CK reference hypotheses. It is not a threat intelligence service, production SIEM, forensic product or real SOAR containment system.

1 · Load samples

Click “Load demo scenarios” to generate a three-incident investigation queue.

2 · Investigate

Open a case to review its evidence, risk factors, IOCs and ATT&CK mappings.

3 · Record findings

Assign an analyst, change status, add notes and download a Markdown case report.

4 · Simulate approval

Request and approve containment simulation. No system-level actions are executed.

Demo data controls

Remove only events marked as demo. Real integration alerts (if any) are preserved.
